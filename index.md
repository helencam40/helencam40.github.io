## 🛠️ Troubleshooting – Quick Fixes for Common Issues

Even the best software can hiccup. Here are solutions to the most common problems.

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| **The app won't start** | Double-check that your download finished completely. Try running it as an administrator: right-click the file and select **"Run as administrator."** |
| **The browser shows a blank page** | Wait 10 more seconds – it might still be loading. Or, refresh the page with the **F5** key. |
| **I get a "Port in use" error** | Another app is using the same spot. Close other finance or server applications, then restart Ghostfolio. |
| **Market data isn't updating** | Check your internet connection. The app needs internet to fetch live prices. |
| **I forgot my username/password** | There is no "forgot password" on your private dashboard yet. Simply stop the app, restart it, and re-create your account. Your data is still there, just under a new login. |
